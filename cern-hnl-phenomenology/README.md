# Heavy Neutral Lepton Phenomenology at CERN

## Overview

This repository documents my 2026 CERN Summer Student project in the EP-Neutrino group, supervised by Dr Animesh Chatterjee.

The project investigated the sensitivity of ProtoDUNE-SP to heavy neutral leptons (HNLs)—hypothetical sterile-neutrino states arising in extensions of the Standard Model. I studied HNL production and decay within the detector, developed a neutrino-background-rejection algorithm, and derived new background-inclusive projected constraints on the active–sterile mixing parameters (U_{e4}^2) and (U_{\mu4}^2).

## Project information

* **Institution:** CERN
* **Group:** EP-Neutrino
* **Programme:** CERN Summer Student Programme
* **Supervisor:** Dr Animesh Chatterjee
* **Period:** June–August 2026
* **Research area:** Beyond-Standard-Model phenomenology, neutrino physics and detector sensitivity

## Work completed

* Investigated the production and decay phenomenology of heavy neutral leptons at ProtoDUNE-SP.
* Developed an event-selection and neutrino-background-rejection workflow using simulated signal and background samples.
* Incorporated residual neutrino backgrounds into projected sensitivity calculations for electron- and muon-flavour active–sterile mixing.
* Presented the analysis at internal EP-Neutrino meetings.
* Authored a summary report describing the motivation, methodology, validation and projected constraints.
