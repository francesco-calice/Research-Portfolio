# Francesco Calice — Research Portfolio

I am an incoming MSc student in Quantum Fields and Fundamental Forces at Imperial College London, and have just finished a First Class BSc in Physics with Theoretical Physics.

My research interests include particle phenomenology, physics beyond the Standard Model, scientific machine learning, and the use of physical structure and symmetry in computational models.

This repository contains selected reports, presentations, figures, and in some cases code from a few of my research and computational projects.

## Selected Research

| Project | Focus | Methods | Available materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Heavy Neutral Lepton Phenomenology at CERN](cern-hnl-phenomenology/) | Background-inclusive projected sensitivity to heavy neutral leptons at ProtoDUNE-SP | Python, GENIE, HNLux, uproot, Awkward Array | No public materials yet available - summary report available by 27th August |
| [Dark-Matter Direct Detection Bachelors Thesis](dark-matter-direct-detection/) | First-principles modelling of WIMP event rates and experimental exclusion limits | Python, numerical integration, Standard Halo Model, Helm form factor, Poisson statistics | Research report, presentation, and selected code |
| [Type Ia Supernovae in Cosmological Simulations UROP](type-ia-supernovae/) | Supernova-rate modelling across large cosmological galaxy catalogues | Python, SQL, A+B and integrated star-formation-history models | Research summary report and selected code |

## Technical Methods

- **Programming and data analysis:** Python, SQL, NumPy, pandas
- **Machine learning:** recurrent neural networks, LSTMs, sequence forecasting
- **Scientific computing:** Linux, GPU computing, HPC workflows
- **Particle-physics analysis:** GENIE, HNLux, uproot
- **Numerical and statistical methods:** Monte Carlo simulation, Poisson
  modelling, parameter-space scans, exclusion limits, and sensitivity
  calculations
